## 🌟 About The Project

**Pixel Office AI** is a personal AI-powered virtual office designed to transform the way people interact with artificial intelligence.

Imagine owning your own digital company.

You are the CEO. Your AI employees work in a virtual pixel-art office, each with their own roles, responsibilities, and tasks.

Instead of managing multiple AI tools separately, you can organize your AI workforce in one centralized environment.

Give instructions to your AI Secretary, delegate tasks to different departments, monitor progress, and manage your virtual company.

**One office. Multiple AI agents. One CEO.**

And that CEO is you.

---

## 🎯 Project Vision

The goal of Pixel Office AI is to create a lightweight, interactive, and extensible AI management platform.

The project aims to combine:

- 🎮 A charming 2D pixel-art virtual office.
- 🤖 Multiple AI agents with specialized responsibilities.
- 🧠 Intelligent task delegation and coordination.
- 💻 Real execution through AI command-line tools.
- 📊 A centralized dashboard for managing the AI workforce.
- 🔌 Flexible integration with different AI engines.

The experience should feel like managing a real company, except your employees are AI agents.

## 🏢 The Virtual Company

The office is designed around a CEO-led organizational structure.

### 👑 CEO — You

The owner and decision-maker of the company.

Responsibilities:

- Give instructions and company objectives.
- Assign priorities.
- Monitor employee activities.
- Review completed work.
- Manage departments and AI connections.
- Control company settings.

### 🗂️ Secretary AI

The central communication and coordination assistant.

Responsibilities:

- Receive instructions from the CEO.
- Understand and organize requests.
- Break large tasks into smaller assignments.
- Delegate work to appropriate departments.
- Track task progress.
- Report results back to the CEO.

### 🧑‍💻 Development Department

Responsible for software engineering and technical implementation.

### 🔬 Research Department

Responsible for research, analysis, and information gathering.

### 🎨 Design Department

Responsible for visual concepts, interface design, and creative work.

### 🧪 Quality Assurance Department

Responsible for testing, debugging, and quality control.

### 📢 Marketing Department

Responsible for marketing strategies, content planning, and communication.

*Department responsibilities describe the intended organizational structure. Actual agent capabilities depend on their configuration and connected AI engine.*

## 🤖 AI Integration

Pixel Office AI is designed to support multiple AI execution engines.

### OpenCode

Integration with OpenCode CLI for coding, software development, debugging, and technical tasks.

### Hermes Agent

Integration with Hermes Agent CLI for AI-assisted tasks and agent workflows.

### Flexible Architecture

The long-term goal is to make AI engines interchangeable through a modular adapter architecture.

This allows different employees to use different AI engines without redesigning the entire application.

> AI integrations require their respective CLI tools, authentication, and valid configurations.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Pixel Office AI aims to deliver a visually engaging experience without unnecessary hardware requirements.

Design principles:

- 2D top-down pixel-art environment.
- Modern office atmosphere.
- Animated AI characters.
- Individual desks and workspaces.
- Clear employee status indicators.
- Organized department layouts.
- Responsive interface.
- Lightweight performance.

The goal is to create an office that feels alive while remaining practical and easy to use.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

Security is an important part of this project.

Development principles:

- Keep API keys and credentials private.
- Never commit `.env` files or secret tokens.
- Avoid exposing sensitive configuration in logs.
- Validate commands before execution.
- Protect access to local application services.
- Keep AI execution transparent.
- Clearly distinguish real AI activity from simulated demonstrations.

**Never publish private API keys, passwords, or authentication tokens in this repository.**
